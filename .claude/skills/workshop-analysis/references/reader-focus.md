# Reader focus

Materialize these exact questions, in order, into `ReaderFocus.questions`:

1. Which frozen EDGE CFP topic does the paper's central contribution address?
2. What efficiency mechanism is proposed, and does it affect training,
   inference, data use, deployment, or an application-specific pipeline?
3. Which baselines, datasets, workloads, metrics, and numeric results support
   the relevant claims?
4. What quality, accuracy, memory, latency, throughput, energy, or compute
   tradeoffs are reported, and under what scope?
5. What hardware, simulator, runtime, and device conditions were actually used?
6. Is on-device behavior measured on physical target hardware, modeled,
   simulated, measured on non-target hardware, or motivational only?
7. Which paper-stated limitations and assumptions materially constrain the
   relevant findings?
8. Which source-supported facts would make a concrete next experiment testable?

Use the ordinary `claims` collection. Do not create workshop-specific output
fields, self-verify claims, score criteria, compare papers, or rank the paper.
