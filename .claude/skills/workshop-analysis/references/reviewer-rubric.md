# Reviewer rubric

Materialize these exact checks, in order, into `ReviewerRubric.checks`:

1. Confirm the accepted population is exactly the authoritative corpus and that
   every entry has one visible terminal identity/source/analysis outcome.
2. Reproduce the top-tier flags from the frozen objective profile and workshop
   ranking policy. Challenge any fixed-k substitution or arithmetic mismatch.
3. Require every ranking reason and factual or relevance statement to resolve
   to critic-supported claims from the same investigation.
4. Make corpus-distinctiveness findings only by comparing all eligible accepted
   papers, citing each material comparison. Label them `reviewer_inferred`.
5. Do not infer quality from long versus short track, proceedings inclusion,
   peer-review metadata, poster number, or author identity.
6. Exclude failed, unresolved, uncertain, unsupported, and overclaimed evidence
   from strong conclusions while keeping every affected paper visible.
7. Keep paper advertising, critic-supported findings, corpus-relative judgment,
   and analyst inference distinct in report wording.
8. Challenge any claim that the public accepted list represents rejected,
   withdrawn, or total submitted papers.
