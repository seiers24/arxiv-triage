# Critic rubric

Materialize these exact checks, in order, into `CriticRubric.checks`:

1. Require the workshop connection to follow from supported claims addressing
   the frozen EDGE CFP topics.
2. Verify every evidence modality and execution environment against the source;
   do not upgrade simulation, proxy metrics, or motivation to hardware evidence.
3. Treat hardware and deployment claims as measurements only when device,
   workload, metric, and execution conditions are source-supported.
4. Preserve baseline, metric, quality tradeoff, and experimental scope in every
   efficiency comparison; mark material strengthening `overclaimed`.
5. Keep missing baselines, ablations, workloads, energy measurements, or
   deployment evidence visible in criterion scores and uncertainty.
6. Apply every frozen objective anchor literally. Score extension leverage as
   an assessment grounded in supported claims plus explicit assumptions, never
   as a paper-stated fact.
7. Trigger human review when degraded source coverage or unresolved environment,
   baseline, metric, tradeoff, or scope prevents a material assessment.

Do not assess corpus distinctiveness. That requires all terminal papers and is
reserved for the corpus reviewer.
